# Storylines — TCS Deccan Park, Waterfall 1 renovation

Source: `TCS_fountain_renovation_BOQ.pdf` (Waterfall 1 Master Guide, BOQ ₹8,35,050 excl. GST).

## A. From a dry rock to four falls (picked)

Device: life before and after, with one diagram that grows. Form: one stage that rebuilds, with a running BOQ total as the carrier.
The viewer sees the real site today (photos from the BOQ), learns how the closed water loop works, then follows the job in work order: one chapter per BOQ group, while a cost bar fills toward ₹8,35,050. It ends on the 3D renders turning 360°, the lights coming on at night, and the total.

- First 2 s: the black-and-white front photo of the boulder, with "Waterfall 1, today" set on a stone-grey placard.
- Beats: site today → problems (wet patch, old cable, dry silt) → how the loop works → the 12 steps → groups A, B, G, C, D, E, F, H → 360° renders, day to night → total + "seal before pipes…".
- Look: concrete-grey ground (#CFCAC2), rock-dark ink (#1F1A17), with water blue (#1E86B8) used only for water and the current group. Oswald for display, Inter for body.
- Sound: a calm bed that opens when the renders turn. Delivered silent for now because no music source is set up.

## B. Follow one litre of water

Device: the worked example. A single drop travels pump → rising main → top pond → side basin → spill lip → lower pond, and each stop opens the BOQ group that repairs that part. It's tighter (~60 s), but electrical, lighting and handover sit awkwardly on the drop's path.

## C. The bill, line by line

Device: type carries the argument. A kinetic ledger runs from item A1 to H2, with the amounts stacking to the total and each large line turning into its picture. It's best for a billing review with TCS, but the weakest at explaining the work.
